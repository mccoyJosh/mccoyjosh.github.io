Chickpea Panzanella

Ingredients:

  • (2 cups) Cherry Tomatoes
  • (15-oz) Can of Chickpeas (Garbanzo beans) (rinsed & drained)
  • (8.5-oz) Can of Artichoke Hearts (drained)
  • Some Red Onion
  • Some bread of your choice
  • Some type of salad dressing (Olive Garden's Dressing is a good choice!)
  • Optional: Basil, salt, pepper, feta cheese, oregano

Instructions:

Set an oven to 350°F. Split up your bread into small chunks. Place on pan and bake for about 10 minutes (enough to get them toasted). This is basically going to act as croutons.

In a big bowl, mix together halved cherry tomatoes, the chickpeas, artichoke hearts, and cut up onions. Add the optional ingredients as well. Once the bread looks good, add it to the bowl as well. Add your desired dressing and mix it all up. Enjoy!
