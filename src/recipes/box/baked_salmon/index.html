Baked Salmon

Ingredients:

Main Ingredient (solids):

  • Salmon
  • Cherry Tomatoes
  • Onion
  • Lemon (~2 per ~1 lb of salmon)
  • Fresh Dill

Other:

  • Olive Oil
  • Garlic Powder
  • Parsley
  • Oregano
  • Basil
  • Black Pepper
  • Sea Salt
  • If no fresh dill, use dill powder; include in the sauce too!

Instructions:

Pre-heat an oven to 350°F.

First, prepare the sauce. Use 1 and a half lemons per pound of lemon for its juice. Add about twice that amount of olive oil to sauce. Add lots of garlic powder, some salt and pepper.

On a baking sheet, add small layer of olive oil and place fish on top of it. Pour about a third of the sauce on the fish; more on the thicker portions of the fish.

Cut up the remaining half of the lemon into slices and place them on the fish. Cut and place enough tomato and onion to cover the top of the fish. Add all the seasoning (parsely, oregano, basil, more pepper) Place fresh dill on top of everything. Pour remaining sauce on everything.

Put meal into oven and allow it to bake for 18 to 23 minutes.

Afterwards, its done so eat it.
