Tomato & Cream Pasta Sauce

Ingredients:

  • ~9.88 oz (280g) jar of Italian Tomato Bruschetta
  • 1 Cup of Heavy Whipping Cream
  • 1/2 (White/Red) Onion
  • A good amount of Garlic Powder lol
  • 1/2 (Orange/Red/Green) Bell Pepper
  • 5-6 Baby Bella Mushrooms
  • 3/4 of 1 TBSP of Butter

Instructions:

1. Cut up the onion, mushrooms and pepper. It doesn't need to be diced per se, but definitely cut up.

2. Throw the cut up onion, mushrooms and pepper into a pan and sauté using the butter. Alternatively, you can use olive/avocado oil. Let the ingredients cook until they seem properly sautéd, i.e. the onions are a golden brown

3. Add the entire jar of brushetta and cup of heavy whipping cream. Go ahead and add the garlic powder to the dish as well.

4. Continue to cook until entire dish is hot and ready to serve.

5. Eat with pasta!
