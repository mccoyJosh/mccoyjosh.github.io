Tomato Soup

Ingredients:

Equal parts:

  • Tomato Sauce
  • Chicken Broth
  • Heavy Whipping Cream

Optional Ingredients:

  • Onions (diced/cut up)
  • Tomatoes (diced/cut up)
  • Spinach
  • Garlic Powder
  • Parsley
  • Oregano
  • Basil
  • Thyme

Instructions:

Put it all in a pot. Add the optional ingredients as you see fit. Heat until boiling or almost boiling... it should be done. Pour and let cool for a bit. Eat it!
