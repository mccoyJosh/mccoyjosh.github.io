Ratatouille

Ingredients:

Main portion:

The goal for the "main portion" is to have equal parts eggplant, zucchini, squash and tomato, so if you get a tiny eggplant, maybe get less of the other ingredients
  • 1 Eggplant
  • 2 Yellow Squash
  • 2 Zucchinis
  • 4-6 Roma Tomatoes
  • Optional: Sausage; this can even be turkey/chicken sausage

Sauce:

  • Onions (diced/cut up)
  • more Tomatoes (diced/cut up)
  • Olive Oil
  • Garlic Powder
  • Parsley
  • Oregano
  • Basil
  • Thyme
  • 2 (Yellow/Red) Bell Peppers
  • Grounded Black Pepper
  • Salt

Instructions:

1. Cut up all the Main Portion ingredients into small circles about 1/4 in. thick

2. Cut up and dice all sauce ingredients and combine them together (easiest if thrown into blender)

3. Slather some of the sauce on to bottom of a pan

4. Place layer of main portion ingredients; ideally, you lay 1 of each ingredient on top of the last in the same order until you run out of space/ingredients. If you want, fill the entire pan and make a second layer; just make sure to add a layer of sauce between the first layer of main portion of ingredients and the next.

5. With all the main portion ingredients out, pour remaining sauce on top of the dish.

6. Put a lid on the pan and let ingredients simmer on a low heat for a long while (~30 minutes if not more).

7. Once everything is tender, it is good! Eat it!
